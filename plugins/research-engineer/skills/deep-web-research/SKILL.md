# Deep web research

Use multiple targeted queries. Prefer official docs, repositories, releases, changelogs, commits, PRs/issues/discussions, then community evidence. Separate verified fact, strong evidence, inference, anecdote, and unknown. Record freshness, trade-offs, risks, and maturity.

Do not treat a search snippet, popularity, or a single blog as proof. Exit with a source-linked evidence table.
